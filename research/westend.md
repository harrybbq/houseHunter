As of 2026-09-29. Source: Rightmove searches (flats, 2+ beds, max £225k) for OUTCODE^924 (G11) and OUTCODE^925 (G12). G11 returned 24 unique listings and G12 returned 9, 33 in total. An OnTheMarket G12 search (2-bed flats, max £225k) and web searches on ESPC and s1homes turned up no current listings that aren't on Rightmove. The OTM, ESPC and s1homes hits under £225k were either the same properties or stale (from 2020 or 2024).

**Headline:** there are **no tenement flats in this patch that pass every hard filter.** The G11 and G12 tenement stock under £225k is all 2nd floor or higher, under 700 sq ft, or has an internal kitchen. What does pass is purpose-built or modern.

Tier definitions (criteria.md names the tiers but doesn't define them, so these are mine):
- **Perfect:** passes every hard filter, is a traditional tenement, is about 850+ sq ft, and has two proper doubles.
- **Strong:** passes every hard filter, and the floor and kitchen are confirmed. It may fall short on a soft preference, such as not being a tenement.
- **Possible:** one hard filter is unknown or borderline.
- **Fails:** misses a hard filter.

## Why
1. **Whittingehame Court (93002565):** This is the cleanest pass. It is on the ground floor according to the text, is 850 sq ft, and the floorplan shows the kitchen has its own window. Bedroom 2 is a real double at 2.84 m. The downsides are that it isn't a tenement, it's in CT band E, and the text puts it on the ground floor but also gives it a balcony, so check whether it's actually raised ground.
2. **34 Addison Road (93547536):** The floorplan shows a large kitchen with a window (3.98 m) and two big doubles. It's on the first floor in a modern block near the Botanics. At 818 sq ft it's a little under target, and it's in CT band E.
3. **1400 Great Western Road (90427002):** This is the biggest in the patch at 982 sq ft on the floorplan. It has a kitchen with a window (floorplan), a bay, and a walk-in shower. The listing doesn't state the floor, and the "Ground Floor" on the floorplan is only the drawing's level label, so ask the agent for the floor and the building type. If it's a ground- or first-floor sandstone flat, it goes up to Strong or Perfect.
4. **Coopers Well Street (93376560):** It has a good size and floor and a double bedroom 2 (2.79 m). The kitchen is a windowless galley that opens at one end into the dining area on the floorplan, so it only just meets the open-plan rule. It's a modern block in CT band E.
5. **60 Dorchester Avenue 1/1 (92399058):** It's cheap at £145k, and the first floor, EPC C and CT band C are all confirmed. The kitchen has a window but is only 2.44×1.88 m. No size is given, and my sum of the room areas puts it at around 740 sq ft, so it's borderline.
6. **Broomhill Drive duplex (93217698):** The floorplan shows the best kitchen here, a dining kitchen with a window. But as an "upper duplex" with communal laundry on each floor, it's almost certainly in the Broomhill multi-storey blocks with an entrance on the 2nd floor or higher. It likely fails on floor.
